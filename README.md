## Overview

**LinkedIn scams** are a growing threat to businesses of every size. Attackers use fake recruiters, cloned profiles, and malicious links to reach employees on a platform they trust. Because these messages arrive outside company email, many security tools never see them.

This guide explains how the scams work and what your team can do to stay safe.

---

## Why LinkedIn Is a Target

Public profiles reveal job titles, coworkers, company news, and reporting lines. Scammers use this to write messages that feel personal and believable. Employees also tend to relax on LinkedIn because it feels professional, which makes it an easy way in.

---

## Common LinkedIn Scams

| Scam Type | How It Works | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Fake recruiter offers | A "recruiter" sends a file, test, or form | Malware, stolen logins |
| Cloned profiles | A copy of a real executive asks for favors | Payment fraud, data leaks |
| Phishing links | A hacked account shares a fake "invoice" | Credential theft |
| Investment scams | A friendly stranger promotes "guaranteed" returns | Financial loss |
| Information gathering | Scammers collect company details quietly | Targeted attacks later |

---

## Red Flags

> [!WARNING]
> Stop and verify if you notice any of these signs.

- New profile with very few connections
- Job offer for a role you never applied for
- Pressure to reply or act quickly
- Request to move the chat to WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal email
- Files or links from people you don't know
- Writing or details that feel slightly off

---

## Employee Safety Checklist

- [ ] Don't open files from unknown contacts
- [ ] Never log in to an account through a LinkedIn message link
- [ ] Verify unusual requests by phone or through IT
- [ ] Use a unique password for every account
- [ ] Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- [ ] Report suspicious messages right away

---

## Business Protection Steps

1. **Train employees** with short, real-world examples.
2. **Set clear social media rules** for work devices and accounts.
3. **Require MFA** on all business accounts.
4. **Install endpoint protection** to block harmful downloads.
5. **Create a simple reporting process** so scams are flagged fast.
6. **Monitor 24/7** to catch unusual logins and activity early.
